# Literature checks

Primary-source checks refreshed on 16 November 2026 (Asia/Dhaka). These are
automated source checks, not a human literature review. The manuscript makes
narrow comparisons and does not claim omission detection, writer/reader pipelines,
or recipient-dependent usefulness as new ideas.

| Source | Checked claim / scope |
|---|---|
| [JANUS v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.10852v2) | Institutional goals and fact-grounded distortion; valence depends on the recipient and objective. Our fixed true-state labels and verbatim selection probe are not its full metric. |
| [Kim et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.656.pdf) | Critical sentences defined by removal-induced reference-reader answer changes; generator-to-reader transmission. A close precedent. |
